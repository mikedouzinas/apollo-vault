# Health & Energy

## Structure

```
Health & Energy/
├── Self-Observations/         — Dated notes on patterns you notice in your own body, sleep, mood, energy.
├── [Something You're Tuning]/ — One folder per active experiment: sleep, nutrition, training, a specific
│                                 condition. Each gets its own file per plan or protocol.
└── [Care Provider]/           — If you see a therapist, doctor, coach, etc., one folder per provider,
                                  with a dated file per session.
```

Rename or drop any of these to match your actual life. Someone not tracking a medical condition doesn't need that folder; someone who is should feel free to add more.

## What Goes Where

| Content | Location |
|---|---|
| A recurring pattern you're noticing in yourself | `Self-Observations/[Pattern].md` |
| A specific plan or experiment you're running | `[Topic]/[Plan Name].md` |
| A session with a provider | `[Provider]/YYYY-MM-DD - Session.md` |

## The point of the folder

This is where the vault earns its keep on anything physical or energetic: it remembers what you tried, what worked, and what a provider actually said, so you stop re-explaining your own history every time.
