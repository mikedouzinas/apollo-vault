# Start here

This folder is a **vault**. It is going to be your memory.

You do not need to read this whole thing. If you want to skip it, open the folder in Claude and say **hi**, and Iris will take it from there.

---

## What this actually is

**It is a folder of text files on your computer.** That is all. You can open it in Finder right now and look at every one of them.

What makes it different from any other folder is a file called `CLAUDE.md`. That file contains instructions written to the AI. It tells the AI to read this folder before it answers you, to write things down as you go, and to keep track of what you are working on.

So: **the folder is your memory, and `CLAUDE.md` is how the AI knows to use it.**

That is the entire trick. There is nothing else under the hood.

---

## Why this fixes the thing that annoys you

You have probably had this happen. You paste a long document into a chat, have a good conversation about it, and then come back the next day and it is gone. The AI has no idea who you are or what you were doing. You start over.

That happens because a chat is a conversation, and conversations end.

**A folder does not end.** When you give Iris something to read, it saves it here. When you work something out, it writes it down here. Tomorrow, next month, it reads all of it again before it says a word to you.

You will not have to manage this. You just talk, and it accumulates.

---

## How to open it

1. Open the **Claude** app.
2. Click the **Code** tab.
3. Click **Select folder**, and choose this folder.
4. Say **hi**.

Iris will introduce itself, ask you a few questions, and set the vault up with you. It takes a couple of minutes and there is nothing to install.

---

## The folders, in one line each

You can ignore all of this and put everything in `00_Inbox`. Iris will sort it. But if you are curious:

| Folder | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **00_Inbox** | Anything, when you do not know where it goes. Start here. |
| **01_Projects** | Work that has an end. A trip, a launch, a paper. |
| **02_Areas** | Work that has no end. Your health, your team, your family. |
| **03_Resources** | What you are learning from. Books, articles, notes. |
| **04_Archive** | What is finished. |
| **05_Attachments** | Files. Images, PDFs, whatever. |
| **06_Metadata** | Iris's own notes: your journal, patterns it has noticed. |

**Nothing is filed correctly on the first try, and it does not matter.** The folders exist so you can find things later, not so you can feel organized now.

---

## The first thing to try

Give Iris something to read. A PDF you have been meaning to get to. A long article. A book.

It will read it, write down what mattered, and file it.

Then **close the window. Come back tomorrow. Ask about it.**

It will still be there. That is the moment where this stops being a chatbot.

---

## Some honest notes

**Your files are yours.** They are plain text, on your machine, in a normal folder. Nothing is locked in a proprietary format and nothing needs this app to be readable. If you ever want to walk away, take the folder.

**You can point any AI at this.** Iris is not magic software. It is Claude, plus a folder, plus a good set of instructions. The instructions are the part that took work, and they are all in `CLAUDE.md` where you can read them.

**You can change how it talks to you.** Open `CLAUDE.md` and edit it, or just tell Iris what you want and ask it to update the file. If it is doing something you do not like, say so. That is how it gets better.

**It will write things down without asking.** That is on purpose, and it is the point. If it ever writes something you do not want kept, delete the file. It is only a file.
